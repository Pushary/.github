# Community conduct

Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions. Critique the work, explain disagreements, and help others reproduce problems. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Email **aadil@pushary.com** to report a conduct problem privately. Include links or context without sharing unrelated personal data. Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to keep the project useful and welcoming.
